한숨

Slow down. We’ll handle the rest.

In Korean, hansum is the breath you let out when you finally relax. That’s the whole idea: good shisha, good drinks, good company — since 2018.

  • Hansum Signature

    Eight house blends, from creamy Jade Bean to woody Black Temple.

  • Your session, your way

    Pick the leaf, the bowl and how strong you like it. Our team packs it for you.

  • Cocktails & late nights

    Signature cocktails, Korean soju and a room made for staying a little longer.
